About Paws Haven

Learn more about our purpose and commitment to animal welfare in Springs.

Who We Are

Paws Haven Animal Rescue is a non-profit organisation concept created to support vulnerable, abandoned and neglected animals. The organisation aims to provide animals with care while working towards responsible and suitable adoptions.


This website is designed to make information easier to access and to give members of the public clear ways to adopt, volunteer, sponsor and contact the organisation.

Our Mission

To help animals in need by promoting rescue, rehabilitation, responsible adoption and community participation.

How We Work

Our mission is carried out in four steps. Select a tab to read more.

Rescuing vulnerable animals

We support vulnerable, abandoned and neglected dogs and cats, giving them a safe place and the care they need. If you know of an animal in need in Springs, please tell us.

Rehabilitation and care

Every animal receives food, shelter and medical attention. Our vaccination programme helps protect animals before and after they find a home.

Responsible adoption

We match animals with suitable, responsible adopters. Browse the animals looking for homes and send an adoption enquiry to find out about availability and fees.

Community participation

Working with the community creates better outcomes for animals. You can volunteer your time or donate to help us do more.
