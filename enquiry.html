Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you send your enquiry.

Our adoption fee is R850 for a dog and R650 for a cat. Choose “Adopting an animal” in the form above to see the exact fee for the animal you are interested in.

Yes. Every adopted animal receives an eligible first vaccination at no extra charge. Annual vaccinations afterwards are a subsidised R150. Read more on our vaccination programme page.

Volunteering is free. Choose “Volunteering” in the form to pick where you would like to help and to see the date of the next orientation session.

You can sponsor from R50 to R500, monthly or once-off. We only share banking details directly with you, so please use only verified Paws Haven donation details. You can also see all the options on the donate page.

We aim to reply within 2 working days. For anything else, please use our contact page.
